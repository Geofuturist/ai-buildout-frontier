# About

AI Buildout Frontier is an open observatory of the physical side of AI:
data centers, the power systems around them, and the constraints on
building them. Every layer can be downloaded, cited by DOI, and rebuilt
from its source.

The question behind it: which data that decisions about AI rely on can
actually be collected from open sources and checked by someone else? The
map is the tool for that audit and its public output.

As of October 2026, the site covers two countries. For the United States:
generation, electricity demand, transmission lines and the interconnection
queue by county, and transmission line segments. For France: generation,
electricity consumption and transmission lines by department and region,
and data centers found in the national register of installations
classified for environmental protection (ICPE). A world data-center layer
and more countries are in progress.

The project is run by Volodymyr Ilin (cited in datasets as Ilin,
Vladimir), an economic geographer and independent researcher based in
Italy. He builds it alone, with AI agents writing the code from his
specifications. Every value comes from a source file or document named in
the layer's metadata; AI agents also re-check samples independently, but
they are never a source of data.
